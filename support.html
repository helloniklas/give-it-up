Promises

How is the day count worked out?
It counts whole calendar days since the promise started, so it ticks over at midnight rather than 24 hours after you made it. The day you start is day 0.
I slipped. What happens when I say so?
The counter goes back to 0 and starts again from today. Your best streak is kept, so you can always see how far you got.
Can I change the wording or start date?
Yes. Open the promise, tap the pen, and edit the text, the start date, or the voice recording.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync, so deleting the app deletes your promises. See the privacy policy for the details.

Voice and notifications

Recording doesn't work.
Check that microphone access is granted in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. Recording is optional; a promise works just as well without one.
What notifications does the app send?
One when a promise reaches a milestone such as a week, a month, or a year. Nothing else. They can be switched off in Settings › Notifications.

Premium

What is Premium?
Your first promise is free. Premium lets you keep as many promises as you like, side by side, as a monthly or yearly subscription.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
On your device, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves access running until the period ends.
How do I restore a subscription on a new device?
Tap the locked button on the New promise page and choose Restore Subscription, signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it.
