I promise

Make a promise to yourself. Watch the days add up. That's the whole app.

What it does

One promise, one number
Write what you are giving up, or taking up, and the app counts the days you have kept to it. Slip, and the counter starts again while your best streak is remembered.
In your own voice
Record the promise as you say it, and play it back on the days it is hardest to keep.
Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no sync, no analytics, and no networking code at all.
